ORDER INSTRUCTIONS

Follow these simple steps to place your HEXA CORE order.

01

Select Your Service

Visit our Home Page or All Services Page and select the service you want.

02

Check Service Details

Carefully check the service name, description and price before placing your order.

03

Make Payment

Check the official JazzCash account details displayed on our website before making payment.

04

Fill Order Form

Enter your name, phone number, sender details and payment transaction ID correctly.

05

Upload Payment Receipt

Upload a clear payment receipt in JPG, PNG or WEBP format. Maximum file size is 5 MB.

06

Submit Your Order

Submit the form and save your order Reference ID for future tracking.

07

Track Your Order

Open Track Order and enter the required details to check your order status.

IMPORTANT NOTE

Always verify payment details before sending money. Your order will be reviewed by the admin. Keep your Reference ID safe.
